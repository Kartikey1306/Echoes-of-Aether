ECHOES OF AETHER (macOS)
Third-person cyberpunk action-adventure, about 45 minutes. Single player.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Double-click the zip to unzip it, then open the "EchoesOfAether" folder.
2. Double-click EchoesOfAether.app. You can drag it to Applications first.
3. The game isn't notarized by Apple, so the first launch is blocked once:
   - macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later: click "Done" on the warning, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
     down to "EchoesOfAether.app was blocked", click "Open Anyway" and confirm.
   - macOS 12 to 14: right-click (or Control-click) EchoesOfAether.app, choose "Open", then "Open" again.
   If macOS says the app "is damaged", run this once in Terminal (adjust the path if you moved the app), then open it:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/EchoesOfAether/EchoesOfAether.app
   After the first launch it opens normally.

The game opens fullscreen and picks a graphics preset to suit your Mac. Fullscreen or windowed and quality can be
changed in Settings > Graphics: Low suits older Intel Macs. A mouse is recommended (on a trackpad, two-finger click is
the right mouse button).

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
macOS 12 Monterey or later. Runs natively on Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and on Intel Macs.
Recommended: any Apple Silicon Mac, 8 GB RAM or more, 4 GB free disk space.
Keyboard and mouse, or an Xbox/PlayStation controller.
Measured at 1080p on an Apple M4: Low 110-210 fps, Medium 90-120 fps, High 50-70 fps. If the game runs slowly, it
lowers its graphics automatically and tells you (unless you have chosen your own settings in Settings > Graphics).

CONTROLS (keyboard and mouse / gamepad)
Move ................ WASD or arrow keys / left stick
Look ................ mouse / right stick
Jump ................ Space / A
Sprint, dash ........ Left Shift (hold to sprint, tap to dash) / B
Crouch .............. C or Left Control / left stick press
Light attack ........ left mouse button / X
Heavy attack ........ right mouse button / Y
Aether Bolt ......... F (hold to aim) / left trigger
Ability ............. Q / left bumper
Ultimate ............ X / right bumper
Interact ............ E / A
Get in / out of car . Return (or E) / A
Switch hero ......... T / D-pad down
Lock on ............. middle mouse button or R / right stick press
Map, journal ........ M, J / Select, D-pad up
Pause ............... Esc or P / Start

Driving: walk up to a car and press Return to get in. W accelerate, S brake/reverse, A/D steer, Space handbrake,
H horn, R reset the car, Return to get out.
Every action except move, look and pause can be rebound in Settings > Controls.
