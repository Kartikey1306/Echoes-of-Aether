ECHOES OF AETHER
Third-person cyberpunk action-adventure, about 45 minutes. Single player.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Unzip the whole folder. Keep EchoesOfAether.exe next to the "EchoesOfAether_Data" folder and the other files.
2. Double-click EchoesOfAether.exe.
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway". The game isn't code-signed;
   this warning appears for most independent games.

The game opens fullscreen and picks a graphics preset to suit your PC. Alt+Enter switches between fullscreen and a
window. You can change quality in Settings > Graphics: Low suits laptops with integrated graphics.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
Minimum (Low preset, 1080p): Windows 10 64-bit, DirectX 11 GPU such as Intel Iris Xe / GTX 1050 / RX 560,
  4-core CPU, 8 GB RAM, 4 GB free disk space
Recommended (High preset, 1080p): Windows 10/11 64-bit, GTX 1660 Super / RX 5600 XT or better, 6-core CPU, 16 GB RAM
Keyboard and mouse, or an Xbox/PlayStation controller.
Based on measurements at 1080p on an Apple M4: Low 110-210 fps, Medium 90-120 fps, High 50-70 fps. Not yet measured on
a Windows PC. If the game runs slowly, it lowers its graphics automatically and tells you (unless you have chosen your
own settings in Settings > Graphics).

CONTROLS (keyboard and mouse / gamepad)
Move ................ WASD or arrow keys / left stick
Look ................ mouse / right stick
Jump ................ Space / A
Sprint, dash ........ Left Shift (hold to sprint, tap to dash) / B
Crouch .............. C or Left Ctrl / left stick press
Light attack ........ left mouse button / X
Heavy attack ........ right mouse button / Y
Aether Bolt ......... F (hold to aim) / left trigger
Ability ............. Q / left bumper
Ultimate ............ X / right bumper
Interact ............ E / A
Get in / out of car . Enter (or E) / A
Switch hero ......... T / D-pad down
Lock on ............. middle mouse button or R / right stick press
Map, journal ........ M, J / Select, D-pad up
Pause ............... Esc or P / Start

Driving: walk up to a car and press Enter to get in. W accelerate, S brake/reverse, A/D steer, Space handbrake,
H horn, R reset the car, Enter to get out.
Every action except move, look and pause can be rebound in Settings > Controls.

SAVES AND LOGS
Saves:   %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\EchoesOfAether\Echoes of Aether\
Log:     the same folder, Player.log (useful if anything goes wrong)
