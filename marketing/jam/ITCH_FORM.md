ECHOES OF AETHER

Two survivors wake in the rain of Aether-9, a neon city that went silent seven years ago. Follow a signal under the
city, fight rogue machines, uncover what Dr. Ilse Maren did to save four thousand people, and decide what to do with
the Core that holds their echoes.

- Play as Kael (heavy close combat with a hard-light blade) or Giva (speed and Echo Sight), and switch between them
  once they reunite.
- Five missions and four side quests across an open cyberpunk district, the metro, a research facility, a vault and
  the Core. About 45 minutes.
- Get into parked cars with Enter, drift through the streets, and watch wrecks smoke, burn and explode.
- Design your hero: presets, body, face, hair, glowing tattoos, glasses and armour.
- Subtitles, rebindable controls, UI scale, flash and shake limits, three difficulty levels. Gamepad supported.

HOW DREAMLAYER WAS USED

- Hero designs: I made one master concept for each hero with DreamLayer, Kael Voss and Giva Vale, and built the whole
  look of the game's heroes around them. The 3D models were made and textured in Blender to match the concepts (face,
  hair, outfit, armour, Kael's holographic forearm interface, Giva's glowing chest core) and checked side by side
  against them in Unity. See the "concept vs in game" screenshot.
- City adverts: six DreamLayer images (ramen, the AetherCore crystal, a cyber implant, a hover-car, techwear fashion,
  an energy drink) play as flickering hologram billboards across the city: the giant crystal over the Arcology Gate,
  the ramen advert above the plaza Noodle Bar and more. See the "adverts vs in game" screenshot.
- DreamLayer made images only; the 3D models, animation, code and sound were made separately.

HOW TO PLAY

- Windows: download the Windows zip, right-click it, choose Extract All, then run EchoesOfAether.exe. If "Windows
  protected your PC" appears, click More info, then Run anyway (the game isn't code-signed).
- Mac: download the macOS zip, unzip it and open EchoesOfAether.app. The first launch is blocked once because the app
  isn't notarized: on macOS 15 or later go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway; on macOS 12
  to 14 right-click the app and choose Open.

Controls: WASD move, mouse look, Space jump, Shift sprint/dash, left/right mouse light/heavy attack, F Aether Bolt,
Q ability, X ultimate, E interact, T switch hero, Enter get in or out of a car (W/S accelerate and brake, A/D steer,
Space handbrake), Esc pause. Gamepads work too. The full list is in the README inside each download.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS

- Windows 10/11 64-bit, DirectX 11 graphics (Intel Iris Xe, GTX 1050 or better), 8 GB RAM, 4 GB free disk space.
- macOS 12 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
- The game picks graphics settings for your computer and lowers them automatically if it runs slowly.
