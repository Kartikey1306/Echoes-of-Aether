## Long description

**Echoes of Aether** is a third-person sci-fi action-adventure set in Aether-9, a research city of four thousand
people that went silent seven years ago in the "Echo Collapse". You play one of two survivors, Kael Voss or Giva Vale,
woken from stasis in the rain beside the Aether Monument in the Central Plaza. A signal is coming from under the city.
Somebody wanted the two of you awake.

Fight the security machines that still patrol the district, follow the signal through the abandoned metro, the
Aether Research Division and the underground Vault, climb to the rooftops, and reach the Aether Core. Along the way
you will meet the survivors' camp, find recordings and lore logs, help the people who stayed behind, and learn what
Dr. Ilse Maren did the night the Core woke up.

### Two heroes, two ways to fight

- **Kael Voss, survey lead.** Close-quarters fighter with a hard-light blade from his forearm interface, heavy
  Aether strikes, the Aether Pulse shockwave and the Core Break ultimate.
- **Giva Vale, systems engineer.** Fast and precise: twin phase-blades, Phase Step, Echo Sight to reveal what the
  Aether hides, and the Resonance Burst ultimate.
- Pick one at the start and switch freely once they reunite. The other hero follows as an AI companion.

### Design your hero

A character designer lets you change body, face, hair, glowing circuit tattoo colour and outfit colours, with presets
and a randomiser, plus 34 hairstyles, 15 glasses, 8 armour sets and 16 glowing tattoo designs.

### A neon city to look at

Haze, neon, holographic ad frames, wet streets and cyber vehicles you can drive (and wreck). Around the plaza, the
city opens out into a 721 x 625 metre district with five areas: Neon Market, Arcology Gate, Kowloon Stacks, Foundry Row
and the Canal Ward, with 15 landmark buildings, skybridges, a canal, pedestrians and traffic.

### Honest notes

- Full voice-over (all 161 lines), on by default; switch it off on the new-game screen or in Settings. Voices are
  rendered offline with the open Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model. All dialogue is subtitled either way.
- All in-game visuals are modelled and rendered in Blender or generated by code. Two DreamLayer concept references guided the hero designs.
- All music, effects and ambience are synthesised by the game's own code.

## Feature bullets

Only items that exist in the code or data of the repository on 2026-10-04.

- Third-person melee and ranged combat: light and heavy attacks, Aether Bolt, abilities, dashes with invulnerability
  frames, lock-on, three difficulty levels (Story, Normal, Hard)
- Two playable heroes with different move sets, switchable after the reunion, with an AI companion
- Five main missions and four side quests across six zones: Central Plaza and Collapsed Market, Abandoned Metro,
  Research Facility, Underground Vault, Rooftop Sector, Aether Core
- Five machine types, from drones to the Aether Guardian boss (drone, sentinel, stalker, warden, guardian)
- Five staged cinematics, 32 dialogues, lore logs, recordings and hidden rooms revealed by Echo Sight
- 12 Aether Fragments, 6 Memory Recordings and 5 Hidden Caches to find; a 12-node Ability Matrix; inventory,
  powerups and upgrades
- Character designer with presets, body, face, hair, glowing ink and outfit sections
- HD characters in cyberpunk techwear with glowing circuit tattoos, cyberware and motion-captured animation
  (CMU Graphics Lab motion capture, 42 clips)
- An open cyberpunk district around the plaza (721 x 625 m, five districts) with pedestrians and traffic, and a kit
  of 10 cyber vehicles
- Three save slots with backups, autosaves and manual saves
- Full keyboard, mouse and gamepad support with rebinding; subtitles with size, background and speaker names;
  adjustable UI scale, flash and shake limits
- Voice-over option (off by default; human recordings only)

## Controls

Defaults from `Assets/Scripts/Core/GameInput.cs`. Everything except Move, Look, Pause, Skip and Debug can be rebound
in Settings -> Controls.

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrow keys | Left stick |
| Look | Mouse | Right stick |
| Jump | Space | A (South) |
| Sprint (hold) / Dash (tap) | Left Shift | B (East) |
| Light attack | Left mouse button | X (West) |
| Heavy attack | Right mouse button | Y (North) |
| Aether Bolt (hold to aim) | F | Left trigger |
| Ability (Pulse / Echo Sight) | Q | Left bumper |
| Ultimate | X | Right bumper |
| Interact | E | A (South) |
| Get in / out of a car | Enter (or E) | A (South) |
| Drive | W/S accelerate and brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake, H horn, R reset | Triggers, left stick, B handbrake |
| Switch hero | T | D-pad down |
| Lock on | Middle mouse button or R | Right stick press |
| Inventory / Map / Journal / Abilities | I or Tab / M / J / K | D-pad left / Select / D-pad up / D-pad right |
| Quick-use powerups | 1 to 5 | not bound |
| Pause | Esc or P | Start |

Note: on gamepad, Jump and Interact share the A button.

## System requirements

- **Minimum (Low, 1080p):** Windows 10 64-bit, DirectX 11 GPU (Intel Iris Xe / GTX 1050 / RX 560), 4-core CPU, 8 GB RAM.
- **Recommended (High, 1080p):** GTX 1660 Super / RX 5600 XT or better, 6-core CPU, 16 GB RAM.
- Based on measurements at 1080p on an Apple M4 (Low 110–210 fps, Medium 90–120 fps, High 50–70 fps; `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`);
  not yet measured on Windows hardware. The game picks a preset from the hardware and lowers it automatically if
  gameplay runs under 40 fps.
- WebGL needs a desktop browser with WebGL 2; mobile is not a target.
